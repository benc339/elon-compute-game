Colossus Tycoon: game design context pack

Last updated 22 Oct 2026 (ET). This is a public context pack for building the "play as Elon" compute-empire game. It covers the vision, the real-world starting data with sources, the current game state, and the 3D-globe plan.

Number rules. Every figure has a period and a source. Tags: - Sourced: a named public source printed it. - Derived: our own arithmetic on sourced inputs. It is never a source figure. - Game assumption: a balance or design value. It is not a real-world fact.

Live game: https://benc339.github.io/elon-compute-game/ (v3). Older versions are at /v2/ and /v1/. Repo: https://github.com/benc339/elon-compute-game

1. Vision (the brief)

  • The game should play like Dyson Sphere Program, in 3D, across the whole Earth.
  • You play as Elon, starting in October 2026 with the real state of the world:
  • Tesla's factories
  • SpaceX's capabilities (Starship, Starlink, launch share)
  • the 13 GW data-center compute plan for end-2027
  • in-house turbine blade and vane casting
  • You compete with rivals (OpenAI/Stargate, Oracle, Meta, Microsoft, AWS, Anthropic, Google, CoreWeave) for chip-ecosystem supply.
  • You order from the real suppliers: NVIDIA, TSMC CoWoS, SK hynix, Samsung and Micron HBM, GE Vernova, Siemens Energy, MHI, aero/industrial turbine makers, Howmet/PCC castings, transformer makers and cooling makers.
  • The tech tree is creative: in-house blades and vanes, modular inference boxes, an own chip, Terafab, Optimus builders, orbital compute, and more.

Agreed design direction: - A 3D globe is the strategy layer. Sites sit at their real lat/lon, for example Colossus in Memphis/Southaven, Giga Texas, TSMC in Tainan, HBM fabs in Icheon and Pyeongtaek, turbine and transformer plants, and the Bastrop casting foundry. Animated great-circle shipping lanes run between them, and rival bids appear as events on the globe. - Zooming into a site cross-fades into a close-up factory view for that site. This is today's top-down v3 factory map, with belts, docks, turbine plants and data-center halls. Zooming back out returns to the globe. - Close-ups stay flat and top-down for now. Fully 3D close-ups are possible, but they are a much bigger build.

That's about as much as I should say about scaling power and cooling."

Source: Elon Musk, SpaceX (SPCX) Q3 2026 earnings call, 20 Oct 2026, from the Motley Fool transcript: https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/10/20/spacex-spcx-q3-2026-earnings-call-transcript/

This was an idea mentioned on the call. No product, size or date was announced.

3. SpaceX/xAI headline facts (SPCX Q3 2026 call, 20 Oct 2026, and Q3 release)

  • Nameplate compute:
  • 1.7 GW at 30 Sep 2026; 1.4 GW at Q2 2026; 0.6 GW at Q3 2025 (SpaceX Q3 2026 results release).
  • "nearly 2.2 GW" at end-Oct 2026, and 2.5–3.0 GW by end-Q4 2026 (CFO on the call).
  • Plan layers. These are different layers, not contradictions:
  • Musk would be "surprised" by less than 13 GW of compute by end-2027.
  • He is "highly confident" of 10 GW added in 2027.
  • There is a "small possibility" of about 23 GW total.
  • There is a +20 GW power and cooling plan for 2027, sized "far in excess of what is needed ... to turn the GPUs on".
  • GPU pace. Musk: "roughly 200,000 GB300s online per month for every month for the remainder of this year and continuing that pace next year. With next year being primarily Rubin, Vera Rubin compute."
  • A later "20,000 GB300s a month" in the same call is treated as a speaker slip.
  • [Derived] Check: +~0.5 GW in about a month at ~2.44 kW per GPU at facility level ≈ 205k GPUs. 20k a month would add only ~0.05 GW.
  • Training cluster: 440,000 Vera Rubin by end-Q2 2027.
  • Blades and vanes: turbine OEMs say blades and vanes run "12 to 18 months behind the rest of the turbine", and "we're casting the blades and vanes for the turbines" (Musk).
  • Capex: Q3 2026 total $19.6bn; Q4 2026 guide $35–40bn (CFO). Space Infrastructure capex was $16,444M in Q3 (release).
  • Economics: new compute has a "payback of less than a year". Pricing is slightly above the high end of $30–50 per watt.
  • Other: Starship Flight 14 reached orbit. Starlink has 13.6M consumer subscribers and 90% of global mass to orbit. Terafab targets a test chip in mid-2027 and volume by Q3 2028 (Fool key-points summary).

4b. What 10 GW added in 2027 implies (all Derived)

These bounds treat the whole 10 GW as VR200 NVL72 ("primarily Vera Rubin"). - Rubin packages: 3.1–3.8M on an IT basis, or 2.4–2.9M on a facility basis. Cross-check: 200k/month × 12 = 2.4M. - HBM4: 0.69–1.09 EB ≈ 5.5–8.8bn Gb in 2027, about 7–18% of our 48.6–75.2bn Gb modelled 2027 HBM demand. - NVIDIA content: ~$400bn. - All-in capex: ~$270–500bn. - Gas turbines: ~340 units if all power were on-site gas at the Southaven mix, which Musk says it won't be ("not just gas turbines"). That means ~52k HPT blades and ~32k vanes. This is an upper-bound illustration, not a forecast. - HV transformers: 14.2 GVA firm / 22.5 GVA installed ≈ 190–300 units at 75 MVA. - Cooling: ~2.8M refrigeration tons; ~5.6k in-row CDUs (GB300-class sizing).

4c. Tensions

  • KGI (15 Sep 2026) expects 5.89M Rubin + 2.85M Rubin Ultra in CY2027. xAI's 10 GW alone would be 2.4–3.8M Rubin packages, 41–65% of KGI's Rubin, before any other customer.
  • This is a tension, not a contradiction: it isn't confirmed whether KGI counts dies or packages.
  • $/GW: $40bn/GW of NVIDIA content would leave only ~$0–10bn/GW for everything else. That is implausible against power and shell costs, and probably reflects IT-basis GW vs nameplate GW.
  • Still unsourced:
  • CoWoS-L packages per wafer
  • Titan 130/350 blade counts
  • whether the ProEnergy 6000PE shares the LM6000 lineage
  • Rubin NICs and transceivers per GPU
  • Rubin CDU sizing
  • SpaceX's GB300/Rubin mix
  • the non-gas share of power
  • OEM slot allocations
  • xAI's actual transformer sizing

5. Turbine and casting supply (the blades-and-vanes bottleneck)

Howmet (HWM): - Gas Turbines end-market revenue was $944M in FY2025 (10-K, 12 Feb 2026). - It was $284M in Q1 2026, +39% y/y (Q1 deck, 7 May 2026), and $321M in Q2 2026, +38% y/y vs $233M (10-Q, 6 Aug 2026). - The company targets going from ~$1B (2025) to $2B+ in 3–5 years (Tech & Markets Day, 10 Mar 2026). - On the Q2 2026 call (6 Aug 2026, secondary transcript), it claimed more than 50% of the global industrial gas turbine (IGT) blade market and said ~24 months pass from committing a new casting machine to its earliest output.

GE Vernova: - Gas turbine run rate is 20 GW/yr in Q3 2026, rising to 24 GW/yr in 2028, with "castings and forgings arriving in '27" (2Q26 call, 22 Jul 2026). It is "mostly sold out through '30".

Siemens Energy: - Company-compiled consensus for Gas Services FY2027 revenue is €16,723m (17 estimates, as of 17 Jul 2026, published 29 Jul 2026). Orders are €32,315m and the margin before special items is 18.3%. - FY2025 Gas Services revenue was 66% service and 34% new units (Annual Report 2025). - New units have "~€400m per GW" of revenue potential (CMD, 20 Nov 2025), and capacity is ~22 GW/yr across all frames (FY2025–27 average, same CMD). - Blades and vanes are its biggest limit. - [Derived] €16,723m × 34% ÷ €400m/GW ≈ 14 GW of new units (≈21 GW at a 50% share). This fits inside the 22 GW/yr capacity.

Other casting supply: - SpaceX's in-house blades-and-vanes foundry in Bastrop, TX was reported at $280M (1 Sep 2026, secondary). - Doncasters IGT revenue was $351M in FY2025, and an industry forecast puts IGT airfoil casting orders at +9%/yr over 2024–2035 (S-1, 15 Jun 2026). - MHI targets +30% gas turbine shipments by FY2028 and has started precision-casting capex (27 May 2026). - GE Aerospace agreed to acquire CPP for $11.75bn (8 Sep 2026). - Wood Mackenzie names single-crystal hot-section blades the industry's critical bottleneck. It puts global gas turbine capacity at ~60–70 GW/yr and the large power transformer lead time at 18–36 months.

Game hook. Howmet's ~24-month furnace lead means its 2027 capacity was largely fixed by mid-2025 orders. Your own casting shop, the Bastrop analogue, is the way around the OEMs' 12–18-month blade lag.

9. 3D tech choice, plan and pitfalls

Search result. There is no open-source Dyson Sphere Program clone or factory-on-a-globe game to fork.

Shortlist: - three.js: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js (recommended base) - three-globe: https://github.com/vasturiano/three-globe, and globe.gl https://github.com/vasturiano/globe.gl, to bootstrap a working Earth quickly and eject later - NASA Blue Marble: https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/collection/1484/blue-marble. Public-domain Earth textures, with no token and no quota. - Rejected: - CesiumJS (https://github.com/CesiumGS/cesium): heavy, with terrain/tile streaming and an Ion token. - deck.gl (https://github.com/visgl/deck.gl): awkward for pickable game objects. - MapLibre (https://github.com/maplibre/maplibre-gl-js): tile-first, with custom 3D bolted on. - Godot and Unity WebGL: 10–40 MB of wasm, poor on phones.

Recommended architecture (agreed in a second-opinion review): - Sim core: - Extract the existing tick loop into a pure JS module with no DOM or three.js references. - Run it in a Web Worker at a fixed timestep (~10 Hz), with a seeded RNG so the headless bot tests keep working. - Post snapshots as pre-allocated Float32Arrays. - Globe = strategy layer: - Sites at real lat/lon in one InstancedMesh. - Lanes as great-circle arcs baked into one BufferGeometry with a per-vertex t attribute, animated by a time uniform in the shader. This gives DSP-style flowing belts at near-zero CPU. - Site close-up = the current 2D map, cross-faded in as the camera closes on a site. This is the single biggest decision. - Rendering details: - Labels are a DOM overlay positioned by lat/lon → screen projection. - Build with Vite and deploy the bundle to Pages, dropping the single-file constraint. - Target 60 fps on an iPhone from the first globe build, with touch controls designed from scratch.

Pitfalls: 1. Labels: cull by camera-facing dot product and distance; cap visible labels at ~40. 2. Per-frame allocation: pre-allocate everything and mutate typed arrays. 3. Texture memory: use 2k textures as the baseline (KTX2/Basis), with hi-res as an opt-in. 8k textures run older iPhones out of memory. 4. Shader scope creep: one Fresnel atmosphere shell is enough. 5. Geo traps: slerp arcs rather than lerp them, handle the antimeridian wrap, and expect flaky picking near the limb. 6. Token-gated basemaps (Mapbox, Cesium Ion): avoid them entirely. 7. Losing the bot tests: split the sim from the renderer first.
